Frequently Asked Questions

What is ContextHire?
ContextHire is an AI-assisted hiring intelligence platform. It provides hiring teams with candidate screening and ranking tools, while providing individuals with resume compatibility analysis and improvement suggestions.
How does the ATS resume analysis work?
The platform parses structured text from resume documents, evaluates formatting consistency, extracts recognized skills, checks for action verbs and quantified impact, and computes a ContextHire Resume Compatibility Score.
Can individuals use ContextHire?
Yes. Individuals, job seekers, and students can use the dedicated Resume ATS Checker to review their resume compatibility score, identify missing keywords, and follow practical suggestions to improve their applications.
Can hiring teams analyze multiple candidates?
Yes. Hiring teams can configure technical job profiles, upload candidate resumes in bulk, and review prioritized rankings and skill overlaps in an organized dashboard.
Does ContextHire make hiring decisions automatically?
No. ContextHire is an AI-assisted decision-support platform. It surfaces insights, rankings, and skill overlaps, but all final hiring and shortlisting decisions are made by human reviewers.
Can I compare my resume with a job description?
Yes. When analyzing your resume, you can optionally paste the target job description to receive specific keyword gap alerts and job relevance matching.
How is my data handled?
Resumes are processed securely in temporary storage for analysis and then scrubbed. Your personal data is never sold, shared, or made publicly accessible.
